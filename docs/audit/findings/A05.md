### A05-001 — Mode B (`net_plus_markup`) adds markup onto `0` unpriced room tiers, publishing PKR 22,000–25,000 Umrah packages
- Severity: P0
- Category: functional
- Where: `lib/domain/marketing-ops.ts:30–54`, `lib/ai/extract-packages.ts:601–604, 710–713`, `lib/validations/marketing.ts:42–45, 149–152`, `app/actions/marketing-ops.ts:485–489, 532, 804–807, 924–927`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): §5.4 "calculates prices across all 4 tiers ... guaranteeing profit margins"
- Evidence: In `lib/domain/marketing-ops.ts:41–54`, `calculatePackageDisplayPrices` unconditionally adds `safeCommission` even when a tier's raw supplier price is `0` (which AI extraction in `lib/ai/extract-packages.ts:601–604` emits whenever a flyer omits `SHARING` or `DOUBLE`, e.g., Quad/Triple-only flyers):
  ```ts
  if (commissionMode === "net_plus_markup") {
    return {
      commissionMode,
      commissionPkr: safeCommission,
      finalSharingPkr: sharing + safeCommission,
      finalQuadPkr: quad + safeCommission,
      finalTriplePkr: triple + safeCommission,
      finalDoublePkr: double + safeCommission,
  ```
  Downstream, `publishFlyerCardToPublicGroupPackages` (`app/actions/marketing-ops.ts:804–807`) and `publishAllFlyerCardsAction` (`L924–927`) copy `pkg.retailSharingPkr` / `pkg.retailDoublePkr` straight into `groupDepartureRoomRates` without filtering unavailable tiers, and `generateMultiPackageSheetForFlyer` (`L532`) computes `const minSharing = Math.min(...flyer.packages.map((p) => p.retailSharingPkr))`.
  Fuzz harness output (`3,003` commission cases):
  ```text
  Mode B with rawSharing=0, rawDouble=0, markup=25000 => retailSharing=25000 PKR, retailDouble=25000 PKR!
  Marketing fuzz: cases=23009, modeBFakePriceFromZero=365
  ```
- Impact (business terms): Uploading any net-rate supplier flyer that lacks a `Sharing` or `Double` column fabricates a live **PKR 22,000–25,000** retail Umrah package on 4K posters, WhatsApp captions (`"Starting From: PKR 22,000"`), and public `/groups` checkout — exposing Al Ramlah to ~PKR 250,000+ loss per passenger or severe customer disputes.
- Root cause: `0` is overloaded to mean both numeric zero and "tier not offered on supplier flyer", and `calculatePackageDisplayPrices` adds `safeCommission` without checking `raw > 0`. Downstream poster/group publishers also assume all 4 room tiers are always available.
- Recommended fix: (1) Treat `raw <= 0` (or `null`) in `calculatePackageDisplayPrices` (`lib/domain/marketing-ops.ts:36–68`) as an unavailable tier, returning `0` (or `null` after schema update) for both `final*Pkr` and `netSupplier*Pkr` when `raw <= 0` instead of `0 + safeCommission`. (2) In `generateMultiPackageSheetForFlyer` (`app/actions/marketing-ops.ts:485–489, 532`), `generateSingleSpotlightForCard` (`L649–652, 694`), and `publishFlyerCardToPublicGroupPackages` / `publishAllFlyerCardsAction` (`L804–807, 924–927`), omit `groupDepartureRoomRates` rows whose retail price is `<= 0`, render `"N/A"` on posters/captions, and exclude `<= 0` from `Math.min(...)`. *(Owner decision needed: confirm whether unavailable flyer tiers should be stored as `0`/`NULL` and rendered `"N/A"` on posters, or require manual entry of all 4 tiers before publishing.)*
- Test to add: Unit test in `tests/domain-and-validation.test.ts` asserting `calculateWrappedPackagePricing({ rawSharingPkr: 0, rawQuadPkr: 280000, rawTriplePkr: 310000, rawDoublePkr: 0, commissionMode: "net_plus_markup", commissionPkr: 25000 })` returns `0` for Sharing/Double and never `25000`, plus integration test verifying `publishFlyerCardToPublicGroupPackages` skips zero-priced tiers.
- Effort: M (<1d)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: yes

### A05-002 — `gemini_ultra_poster` Mode A ingestion stores net cost into `raw*Pkr`, wiping out 100% of profit on first Studio edit (and Mode A clamps `commission > raw` to `0`)
- Severity: P0
- Category: data-integrity
- Where: `lib/ai/extract-packages.ts:572–587`, `app/actions/marketing-ops.ts:1747, 1796–1808, 1908–1932`, `lib/domain/marketing-ops.ts:56–68`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): §1.3 "preventing double-markup or quoting below net cost"
- Evidence: In `lib/ai/extract-packages.ts:572–587` (`ingestionMode === "gemini_ultra_poster"`, invoked by `ingestFinishedPosterOrFlyerAction` in `app/actions/marketing-ops.ts:1728–1815` where `commissionMode: "included_in_flyer"` is stored at `L1747`), the code subtracts `safeCommission` and overwrites `raw*Pkr` with `net*Pkr`:
  ```ts
  if (ingestionMode === "gemini_ultra_poster") {
    // Retail prices are already final on the poster
    netSharingPkr = Math.max(0, retailSharingPkr - safeCommission)
    ...
    rawSharingPkr = netSharingPkr
  ```
  When staff subsequently edits *any* field of that card in Studio (`updateExtractedFlyerCardAction`, `app/actions/marketing-ops.ts:1908–1927`), the form submits `rawSharingPkr` (`278,000`) and calls `calculateWrappedPackagePricing` with `commissionMode = "included_in_flyer"`: `retailSharingPkr` drops from `300,000` to **`278,000`** (selling at wholesale net cost — **0 PKR profit**!) and `netSharingPkr` drops to **`256,000`** (double-subtracted commission!). Additionally, in `lib/domain/marketing-ops.ts:63–66`, whenever `commissionPkr >= rawPrice` (e.g., typo `rawQuadPkr = 18000` vs `commissionPkr = 22000`), `Math.max(0, quad - safeCommission)` silently clamps `netSupplierQuadPkr` to `0` (`91` fuzz hits).
  Fuzz output:
  ```text
  gemini_ultra_poster card (initial retail=300000, comm=22000) => saved raw=278000 => after 1 Studio edit: retail=278000 (profit wiped!), net=256000
  Mode A with rawSharing=0, rawQuad=18000, comm=22000 => netSharing=0, netQuad=0, retailQuad=18000
  ```
- Impact (business terms): Editing a hotel spelling or flight time on an ingested BYO poster card silently reduces all 4 retail room prices by PKR 22,000 (wiping out Al Ramlah's entire profit per pilgrim) and understates supplier net cost by another PKR 22,000 on every save.
- Root cause: `extract-packages.ts:584–587` violated the invariant of `raw*Pkr` under `included_in_flyer` (where `raw*Pkr` must hold the printed retail price so `calculateWrappedPackagePricing` is idempotent on re-edits). Separately, `calculatePackageDisplayPrices` clamps negative differences to `0` instead of rejecting `0 < raw <= commission` in Mode A.
- Recommended fix: (1) In `lib/ai/extract-packages.ts:584–587`, set `rawSharingPkr = retailSharingPkr` (and same for Quad/Triple/Double) when `ingestionMode === "gemini_ultra_poster"` so `updateExtractedFlyerCardAction` is strictly idempotent. (2) In `supplierFlyerUpsertSchema` and `updateExtractedFlyerCardSchema` (`lib/validations/marketing.ts:48–72, 129–155`), reject any positive tier price where `commissionMode === "included_in_flyer"` and `0 < rawTierPkr <= commissionPkr`.
- Test to add: Round-trip idempotency test ingesting a `gemini_ultra_poster` card at `300,000 PKR` with `22,000 PKR` commission, running `updateExtractedFlyerCardAction` changing only `makkahHotelName`, and asserting `retailSharingPkr === 300000` and `netSharingPkr === 278000`.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### A05-003 — Masar SAR visa brackets (`10d/15d/21d/28-30d`) are dead-code bypassed on customer submission & planner, causing 3-way PKR visa total divergence
- Severity: P0
- Category: functional
- Where: `lib/domain/visa-calc.ts:57–88`, `app/actions/applications.ts:102–135`, `components/planner/planner-context.tsx:1557–1573`, `lib/domain/quote-hints.ts:151–181`, `db/seed.ts:175–187`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): §5.4 "accounts for real-world Saudi Masar fee brackets (10d, 15d, 21d, 30d)"
- Evidence: Three distinct pricing paths compute three incompatible PKR visa totals for the same booking:
  1. **Submission (`app/actions/applications.ts:122–128`) & Planner (`components/planner/planner-context.tsx:1559–1573`)**: Neither passes `sarToPkrRate` (and `PlannerProvider` omits `withMasar10DaysSar..withMasar28DaysSar` from `visaRatesTiers`). In `lib/domain/visa-calc.ts:78–82`:
     ```ts
     if (options.sarToPkrRate) {
       pricePerPassportPkr = pricePerPassportSar * options.sarToPkrRate
     } else {
       pricePerPassportPkr = rates.shortTermPricePkr
     }
     ```
     Omitting `sarToPkrRate` discards all 4 Masar SAR brackets (`500/515/530/545 SAR`) and charges flat `42,500 PKR`.
  2. **Admin Quote Hints (`lib/domain/quote-hints.ts:172–177`)**: Passes `{ withMasar, sarToPkrRate: conversionRate }` (default `75`), discards `visaRes.totalVisaCostPkr`, and computes `const totalPkr = totalSar * conversionRate`. For a 2-passport **Visa-Only** booking (`withMasar = false`), the customer's locked price & auto-created `applicationQuotes.visaTotalPkr` is **`PKR 85,000`** (`2 × 42,500`), whereas `rateHints.visaHint.totalPkr` shows **`PKR 70,500`** (`2 × 470 SAR × 75`) — a **PKR 14,500 discrepancy**!
  3. **Internal inversion inside `lib/domain/visa-calc.ts:84–87`**: When `sarToPkrRate: 75` IS passed, `withMasar: true` (`500 SAR`) returns `37,500 PKR`, whereas `withMasar: false` (`470 SAR`) ignores `sarToPkrRate` (`pricePerPassportPkr = rates.shortTermPricePkr`) and returns `42,500 PKR` — charging **PKR 5,000 more** for the cheaper `470 SAR` Without-Masar visa!
  4. **Fallback & Seed corruption**: `applications.ts:109` falls back to `65000` for `long_term` while `planner-context.tsx:1561` and `quote-hints.ts:152` fall back to `68000`; furthermore, re-running `db/seed.ts:175–187` (`W0-001`) overwrites the `long_term` DB row to `pricePkr: 42500, maxDays: 30`.
  Fuzz harness output (`8,075` cases):
  ```text
  delta=  9 (2026-11-10) => days= 10 | bracket="With Masar ≤10 Days (500 SAR)" | masarNoFX=85000 PKR vs masarFx75=75000 PKR vs noMasarFx75=85000 PKR
  delta= 29 (2026-11-30) => days= 30 | bracket="With Masar ≤30 Days (545 SAR)" | masarNoFX=85000 PKR vs masarFx75=81750 PKR vs noMasarFx75=85000 PKR
  Visa fuzz summary: cases=8075, invertedClampedTo1=990, masarBypassMismatches=4000
  ```
- Impact (business terms): Every `With Masar` visa application ignores the 10d/15d/21d/28–30d SAR brackets at submission; `Visa Only` customers receive a locked PKR quote (`42,500/pax`) that contradicts the consultant's worksheet hint (`35,250/pax`); and any re-seed drops 90-day visas from PKR 68,000 to PKR 42,500 (PKR 25,500 loss per passport).
- Root cause: `sarToPkrRate` is not persisted in catalog settings (`visa_rates` or `system_settings`), so `PlannerProvider` and `submitCustomApplication` omit it while `quote-hints.ts` hardcodes `75` and bypasses `visaRes.totalVisaCostPkr`. Meanwhile `db/seed.ts:177` used literal values instead of `sql\`excluded.price_pkr\`` on `onConflictDoUpdate`.
- Recommended fix: (1) Persist an admin-configurable `sarToPkrRate` in catalog/settings (or explicit PKR columns per Masar bracket on `visa_rates`) and pass identical `VisaRateTiers` + `sarToPkrRate` in `PlannerProvider`, `submitCustomApplication`, `submitGroupBookingApplication`, and `calculateApplicationRateHints`. (2) Use `visaRes.totalVisaCostPkr` directly in `lib/domain/quote-hints.ts:175–181` and align `withMasar: false` vs `withMasar: true` FX handling in `lib/domain/visa-calc.ts:84–87`. (3) Unify `longTermPricePkr` fallback to `68000` (`app/actions/applications.ts:109`) and fix `db/seed.ts:175–187` to reference `excluded.*` per `W0-001`. *(Owner decision needed: should `Visa Only` (`Without Masar`) always charge the fixed PKR retail price in `visa_rates.price_pkr` while `With Masar` brackets convert SAR × catalog `sarToPkrRate`, or should all tiers convert from SAR?)*
- Test to add: Cross-layer parity test asserting that for `Visa Only` (`10d`, `25d`, `45d`) and `With Masar` (`10d`, `15d`, `21d`, `30d`), `PlannerProvider`, `submitCustomApplication`, and `calculateApplicationRateHints` compute the exact same integer `totalVisaCostPkr`.
- Effort: M (<1d)
- Confidence: High
