### A11-001 — Raw supplier flyer graphics leak onto public `/` homepage banners and social scheduler assets (`H13`)

- Severity: P0
- Category: security
- Where: `components/admin/hubs/studio-hub-client.tsx:535–542, 1591–1596`; `app/actions/marketing-ops.ts:819, 857, 1745–1748, 1809–1812, 1834`; `components/public/group-packages-section.tsx:254–260`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): §1.1 (`docs/PLATFORM_MASTER_BRIEF.md:27`) & §6.2 (`docs/PLATFORM_MASTER_BRIEF.md:575`) "Keep supplier identities and net wholesale rates strictly private inside the admin dashboard."
- Evidence:
  1. `/admin/studio` (`StudioHubClient`) exposes only a single ingestion dropzone (`Tab 1`), whose Direct Media URL input explicitly prompts staff with placeholder `/uploads/sample-posts/ammaar-raw-17cards.jpg` (`studio-hub-client.tsx:1592`) — an unwrapped supplier flyer containing wholesaler graphics and raw rates — while hardcoding `ingestionMode: "gemini_ultra_poster"` (`studio-hub-client.tsx:538`).
  2. When `ingestionMode === "gemini_ultra_poster"`, `ingestFinishedPosterOrFlyerAction` copies `rawInput.mediaUrl` verbatim into `supplierFlyers.multiSheetImageUrl` (`marketing-ops.ts:1745–1748`), every `supplierFlyerPackages.spotlightPosterUrl` (`L1809–1812`), and every `postAssets.imageUrl` (`L1834`) without re-wrapping or verifying Al Ramlah branding.
  3. When staff click `"Publish to Web"` (`publishFlyerCardToPublicGroupPackages` -> `syncFlyerCardToGroupPackagesInternal`), `groupPackages.bannerUrl` is set to `pkg.spotlightPosterUrl || pkg.flyer.multiSheetImageUrl` (`marketing-ops.ts:819, 857`), which `GroupPackagesSection` renders publicly on `/` via `<Image src={pkg.bannerUrl} ... />` (`components/public/group-packages-section.tsx:255`).
  4. Additionally, `postAssets.searchableText` concatenates `supplier.name` (`marketing-ops.ts:553, 712, 1840`), exposing confidential wholesaler names inside cross-hub post search indexes.
- Impact (business terms): Any raw wholesaler flyer dropped into Studio's only upload form is published directly onto the public homepage and queued into Instagram/Facebook/WhatsApp campaigns with the wholesaler's logo, competitor phone numbers, and un-marked-up net B2B rates visible to retail pilgrims, bypassing Al Ramlah Travels completely.
- Root cause: `StudioHubClient` conflates raw supplier flyer ingestion (`raw_supplier_flyer`) with finished BYO poster ingestion (`gemini_ultra_poster`), and `syncFlyerCardToGroupPackagesInternal` blindly promotes `spotlightPosterUrl || multiSheetImageUrl` to public `groupPackages.bannerUrl` with no guard distinguishing raw supplier media from Al Ramlah-branded renders.
- Recommended fix:
  1. Require an explicit two-mode selector in `StudioHubClient` (`"Raw Supplier Flyer — Wrap in Al Ramlah Template"` vs `"Finished Al Ramlah 4K Poster"`), defaulting to `"Raw Supplier Flyer"` whenever `supplier.defaultCommissionMode === "net_plus_markup"`, and remove raw flyer paths from the finished-poster URL placeholder.
  2. Block `syncFlyerCardToGroupPackagesInternal` (`marketing-ops.ts:819, 857`) from setting `bannerUrl` to any URL equal to `flyer.rawFlyerImageUrl` unless `ingestionSource === "gemini_ultra_poster"` has been explicitly attested as Al Ramlah-branded; auto-trigger `generateSinglePackageSpotlight` before public sync when `spotlightPosterUrl` is missing on `raw_supplier_flyer` cards.
  3. Strip `supplier.name` from `postAssets.searchableText` or restrict `searchableText` strictly to admin-authenticated queries.
- Test to add: Integration test ingesting a `raw_supplier_flyer`, publishing a card to `group_packages`, fetching public `/` RSC props, and asserting zero occurrences of `rawFlyerImageUrl`, `supplier.name`, `raw*Pkr`, `net*Pkr`, or `commissionPkr` in public HTML/JSON or `bannerUrl`.

- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### A11-002 — Mode B (`raw_supplier_flyer` / `net_plus_markup`) UI is completely unmounted dead code, and Studio's Edit Card modal double-deducts Mode A commission (`H13`)

- Severity: P0
- Category: functional
- Where: `components/admin/marketing/supplier-flyers-client.tsx:118–350`; `app/admin/(dashboard)/supplier-flyers/page.tsx`; `components/admin/hubs/studio-hub-client.tsx:538–540, 686–715, 1528, 1793, 2828, 2838–2891, 2915–2931`; `lib/ai/extract-packages.ts:572–588`; `app/actions/marketing-ops.ts:1908–1952`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): §6.2 (`docs/PLATFORM_MASTER_BRIEF.md:567–596`) Dual Commission Modes — Mode A (`included_in_flyer`) and Mode B (`net_plus_markup`, where wholesale net + PKR markup = retail).
- Evidence:
  1. Repository-wide search across `app/` and `components/` proves `SupplierFlyersClient` (`components/admin/marketing/supplier-flyers-client.tsx:118`), `uploadRawFlyerFileAction`, `extractSupplierFlyerDraftAction`, and `upsertSupplierFlyerWithPackages` have **zero callers** outside `supplier-flyers-client.tsx` itself; `/admin/supplier-flyers` redirects to `/admin/studio?tab=studio`, which mounts `StudioHubClient`.
  2. `StudioHubClient` hardcodes `ingestionMode: "gemini_ultra_poster"` and `commissionMode: "included_in_flyer"` on every upload (`L538–539`), stamps `"Mode A: Included"` on the ingestion form (`L1528`), stamps `"Mode A: Comm Included"` on every Vault flyer card regardless of `flyer.commissionMode` (`L1793`), and previews `Math.max(0, editForm.rawSharingPkr - editForm.commissionPkr)` in the Edit Card modal even for `net_plus_markup` flyers (`L2828, 2929`).
  3. **Mode A Edit Double-Deduction (cross-ref `A05-002`)**: During `gemini_ultra_poster` ingestion, `mapAiOutputToResult` (`lib/ai/extract-packages.ts:574–587`) sets `retailSharingPkr = 307700`, `netSharingPkr = 307700 - 22000 = 285700`, and `rawSharingPkr = netSharingPkr` (`285700`). When staff open `"Edit Card"` in Studio (`studio-hub-client.tsx:686–715, 2838, 2917`), the input is bound to `card.rawSharingPkr` (`285700`) while the preview label calls it `"Retail Sharing: PKR 285,700"` and `"Net Supplier Payable: PKR 263,700"`. Saving without touching a single digit calls `updateExtractedFlyerCardAction` (`marketing-ops.ts:1911–1951`) -> `calculateWrappedPackagePricing` (`included_in_flyer`), overwriting `retailSharingPkr = 285700` and `netSharingPkr = 263700` — silently wiping PKR `22,000` per passenger off the retail price on every save.
- Impact (business terms): Staff have no UI anywhere in the app to ingest net B2B flyers with markup (Mode B, e.g., Upsky wholesale sheets), and opening + saving any Mode A card in Studio silently slashes the public retail price by the entire agency commission (`PKR 22,000` loss per pilgrim, `PKR 88,000` on a family of 4).
- Root cause: When `/admin/supplier-flyers` was consolidated into the 4-Hub `/admin/studio` IA, `SupplierFlyersClient`'s Mode A/Mode B state machine was left unmounted instead of merged into `StudioHubClient`, and `mapAiOutputToResult` stored `net*Pkr` inside `raw*Pkr` while `calculateWrappedPackagePricing` treats `raw*Pkr` in Mode A as the gross retail figure (`retail = raw`).
- Recommended fix:
  1. Restore the Mode A (`included_in_flyer`) vs Mode B (`net_plus_markup`) + `raw_supplier_flyer` vs `gemini_ultra_poster` controls in `StudioHubClient` (auto-initialized from `selectedSupplier.defaultCommissionMode` and `defaultCommissionPkr`), and delete or re-export dead `SupplierFlyersClient` once merged.
  2. Fix `mapAiOutputToResult` (`lib/ai/extract-packages.ts:584–587`) so `raw*Pkr` in `gemini_ultra_poster` (`included_in_flyer`) stores the extracted gross flyer rate (`c.*PricePkr`, matching `calculateWrappedPackagePricing`'s invariant `retail = raw, net = raw - commission`), and render `flyer.commissionMode`-accurate math (`raw + commission` vs `raw - commission`) in `studio-hub-client.tsx:1793, 2828, 2913–2932`.
- Test to add: Round-trip unit + action test for both Mode A (`307,700` gross, `22,000` comm -> `retail = 307,700`, `net = 285,700`) and Mode B (`285,700` net, `22,000` markup -> `retail = 307,700`, `net = 285,700`) proving `ingest -> open edit modal -> save unchanged -> save with updated hotel` preserves exact `raw*Pkr`, `net*Pkr`, and `retail*Pkr`.
- Effort: M (<1d)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### A11-003 — `ART-<SUP>-<DUR>D-<NN>` reference codes collide across multi-sheet batches (`23505` crash), fragment into `PKG-...` in background runs, and trust unvalidated AI `postCode` (`H29`)

- Severity: P0
- Category: data-integrity
- Where: `app/actions/marketing-ops.ts:286, 350–355, 450–451, 1763–1778`; `lib/domain/marketing-ops.ts:183–204`; `db/schema/suppliers-and-marketing.ts:212, 276`; `trigger/extract-packages.task.ts:294, 333–366, 392`; `lib/whatsapp/post-ref-detector.ts`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): §6.3 & §6.5 (`docs/PLATFORM_MASTER_BRIEF.md:620–660`) Unique, immutable `ART-<SUP>-...` post reference codes linking Studio posters, `Cmd+K` lookup, and WhatsApp auto-replies.
- Evidence:
  1. **100% Crash on Second Sheet of Same Supplier + Duration**: `supplier_flyer_packages.post_code` has a global unique index (`idx_pkg_item_post_code`, `suppliers-and-marketing.ts:212, 276`). In `ingestFinishedPosterOrFlyerAction` (`marketing-ops.ts:1763–1770`), `generatePostReferenceCode({ supplierPrefix: supplier.codePrefix, durationDays: extraction.durationDays, cardNumber: i + 1 })` is called **without `batchSalt`** and resets `cardNumber` to `i + 1` (`01..N`) instead of preserving printed multi-sheet numbers (`10`–`56` in `sample_posts/upsky-travels/.../1.05.50 PM (1).jpeg` through `1.05.51 PM (2).jpeg`, or `10`–`17` in `sample_posts/ammaar/.../2.png`). Uploading Sheet 2 of Upsky's 6-sheet `21D` WhatsApp batch immediately generates `ART-UP-21D-01` again and throws PostgreSQL `23505` unique violation mid-loop, leaving an orphaned `supplier_flyers` header row (`L1724`) in Neon (`neon-http` has no transaction).
  2. **2-Hex-Char Birthday Collision in Mode B**: `upsertSupplierFlyerWithPackages` (`marketing-ops.ts:286, 350`) sets `batchSalt = flyerId.replace(/-/g, "").slice(0, 2).toUpperCase()` — only `256` possible values (`50%` collision probability after just `19` flyers for a supplier/duration), whereas `generateMultiPackageSheetForFlyer` (`L450`) uses `3` hex chars (`slice(0, 3)`).
  3. **Format Drift Breaks WhatsApp Bot**: `trigger/extract-packages.task.ts:294, 392` mints `PKG-${flyerId.slice(0, 8).toUpperCase()}-01` instead of `ART-...`, which never matches the WhatsApp inbound `ART-...` regex detector (`lib/whatsapp/post-ref-detector.ts`).
  4. **Cross-Supplier Overwrite via Untrusted `postCode`**: `rawAiCardSchema` (`lib/ai/extract-packages.ts:181`) extracts `postCode` directly from flyer text; `marketing-ops.ts:1764` uses `c.postCode` without format/ownership checks, and `trigger/extract-packages.task.ts:333–366` runs `.onConflictDoUpdate({ target: [supplierFlyerPackages.postCode] })`, allowing a newly uploaded or re-uploaded flyer containing an existing `ART-...` string to overwrite another flyer's package prices and hotel names.
- Impact (business terms): Staff cannot upload multi-page WhatsApp rate batches (like Upsky's 6-page 21-day Saudia release or Ammaar's 2-page 17-card release) without crashing Studio on page 2, leaving broken partial flyers in the Vault, or breaking WhatsApp `ART-...` instant quote replies.
- Root cause: `postCode` minting is duplicated across 4 call sites with inconsistent formats (`no salt`, `2-char salt`, `3-char salt`, `PKG-...`), ignores printed `c.cardNumber` (`10`–`56`), and accepts LLM-extracted `postCode` strings without checking supplier ownership or uniqueness.
- Recommended fix:
  1. Centralize `postCode` minting in a single server helper (`ART-<SUP>-<DUR>D-<BATCH4>-<CARD>` using a 4-char CSPRNG/UUID base36 batch token + normalized printed `cardNumber`), keeping `postCode` immutable once inserted (`updateExtractedFlyerCardAction` already preserves `existingCard.postCode` — keep that invariant).
  2. Only honor an extracted `c.postCode` in `gemini_ultra_poster` mode if it matches `^ART-[A-Z0-9-]+$` **and** does not already exist in `supplier_flyer_packages` (otherwise mint a fresh collision-safe `ART-...` code and surface a warning).
  3. Replace `PKG-...` and cross-flyer `onConflictDoUpdate` in `trigger/extract-packages.task.ts:294, 333–366, 392` with the canonical `ART-...` generator scoped to `(flyer_id, card_number)`.
- Test to add: Integration test ingesting all 6 Upsky `21D` sheets (`56` cards numbered `01`–`56`) sequentially under supplier `UP` and asserting `56` unique `ART-UP-21D-...` codes with zero `23505` errors, preserved card badges `01`–`56`, and 100% match rate in `detectPostRefCode()`.
- Effort: M (<1d)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### A11-004 — Arbitrary local file exfiltration, SSRF, and unescaped `--no-sandbox` Chromium HTML injection in poster ingestion & rendering

- Severity: P0
- Category: security
- Where: `app/actions/marketing-ops.ts:1600–1686`; `lib/ai/extract-packages.ts:300–334`; `trigger/extract-packages.task.ts:165–215`; `lib/posters/render-multi-package-poster.ts:45–81, 221–245`; `lib/posters/render-single-package-poster.ts:50–80, 206–300`; `lib/posters/chromium-cdp.ts:74–90, 171–184, 243–246`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): AGENTS.md rule "Validate every Server Action, Route Handler, and form payload with `zod`" & security boundary rules.
- Evidence:
  1. **Missing Zod Validation + Path Traversal / Arbitrary File Read to External LLMs**: `ingestFinishedPosterOrFlyerAction` (`marketing-ops.ts:1600`) has **no Zod schema validation**. Both `loadMediaBytes` (`lib/ai/extract-packages.ts:315–323`) and `ingestFinishedPosterOrFlyerAction` (`marketing-ops.ts:1669–1676`) resolve non-HTTP `mediaUrl` values via:
     - `mediaUrl = "/../.env"` -> `path.join(process.cwd(), "public", "..", ".env")` = `<repo>/web/.env` (zero `path.resolve` boundary check!).
     - `mediaUrl = ".env"` or `"C:\\Windows\\win.ini"` (no leading `/`) -> `localPath = mediaUrl` passed directly to `fs.readFile(localPath)`, forwarding raw server file bytes to Google/OpenRouter APIs.
  2. **SSRF to Public R2 Bucket**: When `mediaUrl` starts with `http://` or `https://` (`extract-packages.ts:304–305`, `marketing-ops.ts:1660–1667`), the server performs an unrestricted `fetch(mediaUrl)` with no hostname allowlist or private/link-local IP block (`127.0.0.1`, `169.254.169.254` cloud metadata, internal Gotenberg/admin services).
  3. **Unescaped HTML / Script Injection inside `--no-sandbox` Headless Chromium**: `buildMultiPackageSheetHtml` (`render-multi-package-poster.ts:45–81, 232–245`), `buildSinglePackageSpotlightHtml` (`render-single-package-poster.ts:77, 223–300`), and `getAirlineBadgeHtml` (`chromium-cdp.ts:75, 89`) interpolate LLM-extracted strings (`makkahHotelName`, `makkahDistanceLabel`, `airlineName`, `daysTitle`, `routePill`, `inclusions`) directly into HTML with **zero `escapeHtml()` sanitization**, then render that HTML via `Page.setDocumentContent` inside local Chrome spawned with `--no-sandbox` (`chromium-cdp.ts:174–175, 243`) or Gotenberg, uploading the rendered screenshot to public R2 (`posters/sheets/*_4k.png`). Prompt-injected text on a malicious supplier flyer (e.g., `<iframe src="file:///...">` or `<script>fetch('http://169.254.169.254/...')</script>`) executes inside headless Chromium and renders exfiltrated secrets onto the public PNG.
- Impact (business terms): A compromised staff session or a crafted supplier flyer image containing prompt-injected HTML can exfiltrate production `.env` secrets, internal cloud metadata, or local files onto public Cloudflare R2 URLs or external model logs, and even benign hotel names containing `&` or `<` corrupt poster DOM layout.
- Root cause: `ingestFinishedPosterOrFlyerAction` skipped Zod/URL boundary validation to allow convenient `/uploads/sample-posts/...` local test paths, and HTML poster templates concatenated untrusted AI strings directly instead of HTML-escaping them and restricting Chromium network/file access.
- Recommended fix:
  1. Validate `ingestFinishedPosterOrFlyerAction` (and all `marketing-ops.ts` actions lacking Zod: `L150, 429, 608, 959, 992, 1600`) with strict Zod schemas. Allow `mediaUrl` **only** when it is either (a) an `https://` URL matching the configured `R2_PUBLIC_URL` host (rejecting private/loopback/link-local IPs) or (b) a normalized path strictly inside `path.resolve(process.cwd(), "public", "uploads")` verified via `resolved.startsWith(uploadsDir + path.sep)`.
  2. Pass every dynamic text/attribute field in `render-multi-package-poster.ts`, `render-single-package-poster.ts`, and `chromium-cdp.ts` through a strict `escapeHtml()` helper (`&`, `<`, `>`, `"`, `'`).
  3. Inject `<meta http-equiv="Content-Security-Policy" content="default-src 'none'; img-src data:; style-src 'unsafe-inline';">` into both poster HTML `<head>` blocks and block `file://` / outbound network requests via CDP `Fetch.enable` or `--host-resolver-rules="MAP * ~NOTFOUND , EXCLUDE 127.0.0.1"`.
- Test to add: Security unit tests asserting (1) `mediaUrl` values `"/../.env"`, `".env"`, `"http://169.254.169.254/latest/meta-data"`, and `"http://127.0.0.1:3000"` are rejected with validation errors before `fs.readFile`/`fetch`, and (2) hotel names containing `<img src=x onerror=alert(1)> & <iframe src="file:///etc/passwd">` are escaped to literal entities in `buildMultiPackageSheetHtml` and `buildSinglePackageSpotlightHtml`.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no
