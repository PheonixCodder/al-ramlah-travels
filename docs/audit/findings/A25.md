## Executive Summary

Al Ramlah Travels presents polished `/privacy` and `/terms` pages designed around Meta and TikTok OAuth App Review checklists (`app/(public)/privacy/page.tsx:107-135`), rather than the **actual data flows, sub-processors, and Pakistani travel-agency statutory obligations** operating inside the codebase. Comparing every legal clause line-by-line against production code paths uncovered **14 findings (`4 P0`, `6 P1`, `3 P2`, `1 P3`)**:

1. **Active Material Misrepresentations in `/privacy` (`P0`)**:
   - `/privacy` §6 (`privacy/page.tsx:148-150`) promises that passport copies and visa documents are stored in *"secure encrypted cloud storage with restricted staff-only access controls"*, while the WhatsApp bot (`lib/whatsapp/auto-responder.ts:366`) and Hajj portal (`components/public/hajj-inquiry-section.tsx:944`) actively instruct pilgrims to send passport and CNIC photos over WhatsApp — where `lib/whatsapp/media-mirror.ts:180-199` and `lib/storage/r2.ts:187-190,243-258` publish them directly to **unauthenticated world-readable `R2_PUBLIC_URL` links** (`A25-001`).
   - `/privacy` discloses **zero AI/LLM processors**, yet `lib/ai/quoting-copilot.ts:1504-1601,1718-1742` and `lib/whatsapp/intent-classifier.ts:240-278` transmit pilgrim names, phone numbers, special medical/mobility notes (including Hajj `bloodGroup`), and up to **15 raw WhatsApp chat messages** (including personal chats on the Coexistence line) to **OpenRouter** and **Google Gemini** without consent or Zero-Data-Retention headers (`A25-002`).
   - `/privacy` §4 (`privacy/page.tsx:107-113`) claims the agency uses *"the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API"* and promises *"You may opt out of promotional WhatsApp broadcasts at any time by replying 'STOP'"*. In reality, `lib/whatsapp/linked-device.ts` runs an **undisclosed unofficial `@whiskeysockets/baileys` WebSocket scraper** (`A25-005`), and replying `"STOP"` has **no global opt-out handler** — instead `unlockOutreachCooldownForPhone()` (`lib/whatsapp/post-ref-detector.ts:145-157`) treats `"STOP"` as an active inbound reply (`replied_active`), **unlocking** the contact for further 14-day sales outreach while Meta broadcast creation (`app/actions/whatsapp-crm.ts:1127-1210`) ignores `do_not_contact` (`A25-003`).
2. **Real Client PII Committed to Public Static Web Root (`P0`)**:
   - `public/uploads/sample-posts/zahoor-adil-rafi-quotation.pdf` (`452,292` bytes) is committed to Git and publicly served at `/uploads/sample-posts/zahoor-adil-rafi-quotation.pdf`, exposing a named multi-pilgrim family quotation dossier (`A25-004`, cross-referencing `A13`).
3. **Pakistan Travel Agency & Hajj Regulatory Non-Compliance (`P1`)**:
   - Zero **Pakistan Department of Tourist Services (DTS)** travel agency license number, zero **Ministry of Religious Affairs & Interfaith Harmony (MoRA) Hajj Group Organizer (HGO)** enrollment number, zero **IATA** number, and zero **FBR NTN** appear on any webpage, quotation PDF, or booking voucher (`A25-006`).
   - The Hajj portal (`hajj-inquiry-section.tsx:61-370,1366-1373`) commercially solicits 10 priced **Hajj 2027** packages (`PKR 1,700,000`–`PKR 2,990,000`) with a `"Confirm & Reserve Hajj 2027 Seat"` CTA and `"Guaranteed Guidance for the Lifetime Obligation"` (`db/seed.ts:2232`) with **zero MoRA Hajj Policy quota / non-binding pre-registration disclaimer** (`A25-006`).
   - `/refund-policy` does not exist (`404`), and `/terms` lacks cancellation fee tiers, group seat deposit forfeiture rules, Saudi visa-rejection liability terms, governing law/jurisdiction (Lahore courts), and consistent passport-validity rules (`6 months` vs `8 months` vs `December 2027`) (`A25-007`).
4. **Fabricated Social Proof, Conflicting Entity/NAP/IBAN Claims & Seeded Scarcity (`P1`/`P2`, `H35` & `§11.10`)**:
   - Because `db/seed.ts` seeds `0` rows into `testimonials`, `components/public/marketing-sections.tsx:103-131,178-179,465-501` **100% of the time** renders 3 hardcoded fabricated 5-star reviews under the badge `"Authentic Pilgrim Reviews"` alongside unsubstantiated `"15,000+ Pilgrims Served Since 2014"` hero counters (`hero-slider.tsx:203-206`) (`A25-009`).
   - Four different legal entity names, 5 conflicting office locations (`Askari 10 House no 38` vs `Office no 38` vs `Commercial Market Sector F` vs `Chaklala Scheme 3 Rawalpindi` vs `DHA Phase 5 Lahore`), 4 conflicting phone numbers (`03274029701`, `03190462638`, `+92 300 8451122`, `03214629700`), and an unverified hardcoded Meezan Bank IBAN (`PK42MEZN...` in `lib/whatsapp/auto-responder.ts:425-428`) create acute wire-fraud, regulatory, and Local SEO (`NAP`) liability (`A25-010`, `A25-011`).
