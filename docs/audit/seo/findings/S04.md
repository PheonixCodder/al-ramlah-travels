## 1. Executive Summary & Exact Character-Count Matrix

Direct measurement of every `metadata` object in the repository (`HEAD 6c54d4c`) reveals systematic CTR and social-card gaps:
1. **Title & Description Length / Keyword Placement**: Only `1 of 4` indexable routes (`/privacy`) currently satisfies **both** the `50–60` char `<title>` target and the `150–160` char `<meta name="description">` target simultaneously. The homepage (`/`) inherits `app/layout.tsx`'s `132`-char slogan-led description (`18` chars short) and brand-first title (`"Al Ramlah Travels — ..."` instead of leading with primary commercial keywords `"Umrah Packages Pakistan"`), while `title.template` in `app/layout.tsx:46` is `"%s"` (a no-op!) instead of `"%s | Al Ramlah Travels"`.
2. **Broken OpenGraph Image Geometry (`680×610` `public/logo.jpg`)**: `app/layout.tsx:64-69` points `openGraph.images` at `["/logo.jpg"]` with zero `width`, `height`, or `alt` metadata. Inspecting `public/logo.jpg` binary headers confirms it is **`680×610` px (`1.11:1` near-square, `28,943` bytes)** — violating the `1200×630` (`1.91:1`) Open Graph / WhatsApp / Facebook / LinkedIn large-card specification. Because WhatsApp is Al Ramlah Travels' #1 lead-closing channel, every link shared by consultants or pilgrims renders as a tiny cropped square logo thumbnail instead of a luxury Haramain `1200×630` preview card!
3. **Zero `twitter` Card Metadata & Zero `opengraph-image.tsx`**: `twitter` metadata (`card: "summary_large_image"`) appears **`0` times** in the entire codebase, child pages (`/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refund-policy`) omit `openGraph` overrides (leaking the homepage OG title/description when shared!), and `app/opengraph-image.tsx` does not exist.

## 2. Detailed Findings

### S04-001 — Root `app/layout.tsx` uses no-op `title.template: "%s"`, `132`-char slogan description (`<150`), obsolete `keywords` meta, and omits `robots.googleBot` `max-image-preview: "large"`
- Severity: P1
- Category: seo
- Where: `app/layout.tsx:44-70`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None.
- Evidence:
  - `app/layout.tsx:44-47` sets:
    ```ts
    title: {
      default: "Al Ramlah Travels — Bespoke Umrah, Hajj & Haramain Journeys",
      template: "%s",
    },
    ```
    Setting `template: "%s"` disables automatic brand suffixing across child pages, forcing every child route to manually hardcode `| Al Ramlah Travels` (or risk omitting the brand entirely).
  - `app/layout.tsx:48-49` sets `description` to `132` characters (`"Where Purpose Meets Excellence — Hajj 2027, Custom Umrah Packages, Flights, Haramain Hotels & Ground Transport by Al Ramlah Travels."`), falling `18` characters short of the `150–160` char SERP target and wasting the first `33` characters on a generic tagline instead of an action-oriented commercial hook + CTA.
  - `app/layout.tsx:50-59` populates `<meta name="keywords">` (ignored by Google since 2009 and exposes competitor keyword targeting), while **omitting** `robots: { index: true, follow: true, googleBot: { index: true, follow: true, "max-image-preview": "large", "max-snippet": -1, "max-video-preview": -1 } }` — a critical directive required for Google Discover large thumbnails and Google AI Overviews full-snippet extraction (`ai-search-geo-and-crawlers.md` §2 & `nextjs-technical-seo-and-metadata.md` §1).
- Impact (business terms): Lower organic click-through rate (CTR) on Google SERPs due to brand-first/slogan-first snippets, truncated image previews in Google Mobile Search / Discover without `max-image-preview:large`, and restricted AI Overview snippet extraction without `max-snippet:-1`.
- Root cause: Initial root layout metadata was written manually without a reusable SEO metadata builder or SERP character-length validation.
- Recommended fix:
  1. In `app/layout.tsx`, configure `title: { default: "Umrah Packages Pakistan & Hajj 2027 | Al Ramlah Travels", template: "%s | Al Ramlah Travels" }` (`default` = **`55` chars**, primary keyword `"Umrah Packages Pakistan"` at char `1`!) — and update child pages managed via `buildPublicMetadata()` to pass concise page titles (or `title.absolute` when `| Al Ramlah Travels` is already appended so it is never doubled!).
  2. Upgrade the default `description` to `154–158` chars starting with an action verb + specifics + CTA, e.g.:
     `"Book custom Umrah packages from Lahore, Karachi & Islamabad, Saudi Umrah visas, 5-star Haramain hotels & Hajj 2027 tours with Al Ramlah Travels today."` (**`150` chars**) or `"Plan custom Umrah packages from Pakistan, instant Saudi visa quotes, steps-from-Haram Makkah & Madinah hotels, and Hajj 2027 tours with Al Ramlah Travels."` (**`154` chars**).
  3. Remove `keywords` (`L50-59`) and add `robots: { index: true, follow: true, googleBot: { index: true, follow: true, "max-video-preview": -1, "max-image-preview": "large", "max-snippet": -1 } }`.
- Test to add: Unit test in `tests/unit/seo/metadata-ctr.test.ts` asserting `app/layout.tsx` `metadata` has `50 <= defaultTitle.length <= 60`, `150 <= description.length <= 160`, `keywords === undefined`, and `robots.googleBot["max-image-preview"] === "large"`.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### S04-002 — Homepage `app/(public)/page.tsx` exports zero dedicated `metadata` and rotates its sole `<h1>` every 7s across generic carousel copy lacking `"Umrah Packages"` / `"Pakistan"`
- Severity: P1
- Category: seo
- Where: `app/(public)/page.tsx:1-60`, `components/public/hero-slider.tsx:25-62,193-198`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None.
- Evidence:
  - `app/(public)/page.tsx` exports `revalidate = 300` (`L26`) and `default async function PublicLandingPage()`, **with zero `metadata` or `generateMetadata()` export**. Thus `/` lacks `alternates: { canonical: "/" }`, `openGraph.url: "/"`, and homepage-specific OG/Twitter copy.
  - Furthermore, the **only `<h1>` on `/`** lives inside `"use client"` `HeroSlider` (`components/public/hero-slider.tsx:193-198`):
    ```tsx
    <h1 key={`title-${current}`} className="...">
      {slide.title}
    </h1>
    ```
    When DB `heroSlides` are empty (`FALLBACK_SLIDES` at `L28`), the SSR `<h1>` is `"Sacred Journeys Crafted with Dignity & Comfort"` (or whatever arbitrary promo headline staff type into `/admin/catalog` CMS Hero Slides!), containing **zero** occurrences of `"Umrah"`, `"Hajj"`, `"Pakistan"`, or `"Lahore"` — and then React unmounts and replaces the `<h1>` DOM node every `7,000 ms` (`L92`)!
- Impact (business terms): The domain's highest-authority URL (`/`) has weak `<title>` <-> `<h1>` topical alignment for high-volume commercial queries (*"Umrah packages Pakistan"*, *"Umrah travel agency Lahore"*), forfeiting top-3 rankings to competitors whose homepage `<h1>` explicitly states their core commercial offering.
- Root cause: Hero slider visual headline was coupled directly to the semantic document `<h1>` without separating visual promo display typography from persistent semantic heading hierarchy, and `app/(public)/page.tsx` relied passively on `app/layout.tsx`.
- Recommended fix:
  1. Export explicit `metadata` via `buildPublicMetadata({ path: "/", ... })` in `app/(public)/page.tsx` (`50–60` char keyword-first title, `150–160` char action-led description, `alternates.canonical: "/"`, full `openGraph` + `twitter`).
  2. Ensure `/` renders a stable, non-rotating primary `<h1>` containing the core entity & commercial intent (either via the persistent eyebrow/headline in the Hero SSR shell — e.g. `<h1>Umrah Packages, Saudi Visas & Hajj 2027 Journeys from Pakistan</h1>` styled cleanly above/within the hero while rotating slide headlines use `<p>` / `<h2>` display styling — so CMS slide edits never accidentally wipe out the homepage `<h1>` keyword signal).
- Test to add: Unit/SSR render test asserting `app/(public)/page.tsx` exports explicit `metadata` with `alternates.canonical === "/"` and renders exactly one stable `<h1>` containing `"Umrah"`.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### S04-003 — Out-of-range meta titles/descriptions on `/terms` (`143` chars) and `/refund-policy` (`48`-char title, `146`-char description) + child routes leaking root homepage `openGraph` title/description
- Severity: P2
- Category: seo
- Where: `app/(public)/privacy/page.tsx:11-15`, `app/(public)/terms/page.tsx:10-14`, `app/(public)/refund-policy/page.tsx:11-15`, `app/(public)/voucher/[token]/page.tsx:23-31`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None.
- Evidence:
  - **Exact character length misses**:
    - `app/(public)/terms/page.tsx:12-13`: `description` is **`143` chars** (`7` chars below `150–160` target).
    - `app/(public)/refund-policy/page.tsx:12`: `title` (`"Cancellation & Refund Policy | Al Ramlah Travels"`) is **`48` chars** (`2` chars below `50–60` target).
    - `app/(public)/refund-policy/page.tsx:13-14`: `description` is **`146` chars** (`4` chars below `150–160` target).
  - **Next.js shallow `metadata` merge trap on `openGraph`**: In Next.js App Router, `metadata` objects merge **shallowly** per top-level key (`nextjs-technical-seo-and-metadata.md` §2). Because `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refund-policy`, and `/voucher/[token]` set `title` and `description` **without** setting `openGraph`, Next.js inherits `app/layout.tsx`'s `openGraph` object verbatim — meaning `<meta property="og:title">` on `/refund-policy` outputs `"Al Ramlah Travels — Bespoke Umrah, Hajj & Haramain Journeys"` instead of the refund policy title!
- Impact (business terms): When staff or pilgrims share `/refund-policy`, `/terms`, or `/privacy` on WhatsApp during booking consultations, the WhatsApp preview card shows the generic homepage marketing title instead of the specific policy document, confusing customers during deposit decisions; SERP snippets on `/terms` and `/refund-policy` also under-utilize the `150–160` char description window.
- Root cause: Child pages exported bare `{ title, description }` literals instead of calling a shared `buildPublicMetadata()` helper that synchronizes `title`, `description`, `alternates.canonical`, `openGraph`, and `twitter` together.
- Recommended fix:
  1. Refactor `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refund-policy` (and all new Phase B routes) to call `buildPublicMetadata({ path, title, description })`, which automatically populates matching `openGraph: { title, description, url: path, siteName: "Al Ramlah Travels", locale: "en_PK", type: "website" }` and `twitter: { card: "summary_large_image", title, description }`.
  2. Adjust lengths into exact target windows:
     - `/terms` description -> `152–158` chars (e.g., `"Read official Al Ramlah Travels booking terms covering Saudi Umrah visa rules, custom pilgrimage quotes, group seats, and Hajj 2027 pre-registrations."` = **`150` chars**; or **`155` chars** with `"in PKR"`).
     - `/refund-policy` title -> `50–60` chars (e.g., `"Cancellation & Refund PolicyPKR" -> "Umrah & Hajj Cancellation & Refund Policy"` via template = **`55–59` chars**, e.g. `"Booking Cancellation & Refund Policy | Al Ramlah Travels"` = **`56` chars**).
     - `/refund-policy` description -> `152–158` chars (e.g., `"Review our official cancellation schedule, non-refundable Saudi visa and airline rules, Ramadan/Hajj blackout terms, and PKR refund timelines in detail."` = **`152` chars**).
- Test to add: Automated metadata contract test `tests/unit/seo/metadata-ctr.test.ts` iterating over every indexable public route (`/` + legal + all Phase B hubs/spokes) and asserting: (a) rendered `<title>` length is strictly `50..60` chars, (b) `description` length is strictly `150..160` chars, and (c) `openGraph.title` and `twitter.title` match the route's own title rather than falling back to root.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### S04-004 — `openGraph.images` points to `680×610` `public/logo.jpg` instead of `1200×630` (`1.91:1`) and omits `width`, `height`, `alt`, `type`, `locale`, `siteName`, and `url`
- Severity: P1
- Category: seo
- Where: `app/layout.tsx:64-69`, `public/logo.jpg`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None.
- Evidence:
  - `app/layout.tsx:64-69`:
    ```ts
    openGraph: {
      title: "Al Ramlah Travels — Bespoke Umrah, Hajj & Haramain Journeys",
      description:
        "Where Purpose Meets Excellence — Hajj 2027, Custom Umrah Packages, Flights, Haramain Hotels & Ground Transport.",
      images: ["/logo.jpg"],
    },
    ```
  - Binary inspection of `c:\dev\al-ramlah-travels\web\public\logo.jpg` (`28,943 B`) shows dimensions **`680 × 610` px** (`1.115:1` aspect ratio). OpenGraph large link previews require **`1200 × 630` px** (`1.91:1` aspect ratio).
  - Furthermore, `openGraph` omits `width`, `height`, `alt`, `type: "website"`, `locale: "en_PK"`, `siteName: "Al Ramlah Travels"`, and `url`. Without explicit `width` and `height` tags (`og:image:width`, `og:image:height`), WhatsApp and Facebook crawlers frequently fail to render an image preview on the **first** share of a URL while asynchronously fetching image dimensions!
- Impact (business terms): Every website link sent to pilgrims over WhatsApp Cloud API inbox, WhatsApp broadcasts, Facebook Pages, or LinkedIn renders either a degraded miniature square icon or a cropped logo instead of a high-converting luxury Haramain `1200×630` card — directly hurting click-throughs on the agency's primary sales channel.
- Root cause: `public/logo.jpg` was used as a quick placeholder for `openGraph.images` without generating a `1200×630` OpenGraph card or populating full OG protocol fields.
- Recommended fix:
  1. Remove `images: ["/logo.jpg"]` from `app/layout.tsx` once `app/opengraph-image.tsx` (`1200×630`, `S04-006`) is added (Next.js App Router automatically wires `app/opengraph-image.tsx` with exact `1200×630` `og:image:width`, `og:image:height`, `og:image:type`, and `og:image:alt` metadata — or explicitly reference `{ url: "/opengraph-image", width: 1200, height: 630, alt: "Al Ramlah Travels — Bespoke Umrah, Saudi Visa & Hajj 2027 Journeys" }` inside `buildPublicMetadata()` so child `openGraph` objects preserve the `1200×630` card!).
  2. Always populate `type: "website"`, `locale: "en_PK"`, `siteName: "Al Ramlah Travels"`, and `url` in `buildPublicMetadata()`. (Keep `public/logo.jpg` strictly for navbar/footer emblem and JSON-LD `Organization.logo`.)
- Test to add: Unit test in `tests/unit/seo/metadata-ctr.test.ts` asserting zero public routes reference `"/logo.jpg"` in `openGraph.images` or `twitter.images`, and every route's `openGraph` specifies `width: 1200`, `height: 630`, non-empty `alt`, `siteName: "Al Ramlah Travels"`, `locale: "en_PK"`, and `type`.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no
