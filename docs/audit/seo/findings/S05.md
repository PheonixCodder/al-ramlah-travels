## 3. Detailed Findings

### S05-001 — All 7 commercial verticals are crammed onto `/` behind non-indexable `#hash` fragments with zero dedicated spoke routes
- Severity: P1
- Category: seo
- Where: `web/app/(public)/page.tsx:56–152`, `web/app/(public)/page.tsx:330–376`, `web/app/(public)/` (only `/`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refund-policy`, `/voucher/[token]` exist)
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None
- Evidence: Directory inspection of `web/app/(public)/` confirms only 5 `page.tsx` files exist (`page.tsx`, `privacy/page.tsx`, `terms/page.tsx`, `refund-policy/page.tsx`, `voucher/[token]/page.tsx`). Requesting `/umrah-packages`, `/hajj-2027`, `/umrah-visa`, `/hotels/makkah`, `/hotels/madinah`, `/ziyarat`, `/about`, `/faq`, or `/contact` hits `web/app/not-found.tsx` (`404`). Meanwhile, `web/app/(public)/page.tsx:57–151` executes 13 parallel database queries on every homepage render and mounts `#journey-builder` (`L336`), `#group-packages` (`L345`), `#hajj-inquiry` (`L354`), `#track-status` (`L361`), `#ziyarat` + FAQs (`L366`), and `#contact` (`L375`) onto the single `/` URL. Furthermore, `HajjInquirySection`, `MarketingSections` (Ziyarat + FAQs), `StatusTracker`, and `ContactSection` are wrapped in `next/dynamic` (`L24–46`).
- Impact (business terms): Search engines strip `#fragment` identifiers (`/#hajj-inquiry` and `/#group-packages` canonicalize to `/`) and assign one primary topical vector per URL. Al Ramlah Travels cannot rank simultaneously for `"Umrah packages from Lahore"`, `"Hajj 2027 pre registration Pakistan"`, `"Saudi Umrah visa price Pakistan"`, `"hotels near Masjid al-Haram"`, and `"Madinah Ziyarat places"` from a single homepage URL, forfeiting almost all non-branded organic pilgrim acquisition and AI Overview citations.
- Root cause: Initial MVP built a single-page landing experience using in-page scroll anchors instead of App Router server-rendered hub-and-spoke routes.
- Recommended fix: Implement the 18-route App Router Hub-and-Spoke architecture in `web/app/(public)/` (`umrah-packages/page.tsx`, `umrah-packages/[slug]/page.tsx`, `umrah-packages/from-[city]/page.tsx` restricted via `generateStaticParams` + `dynamicParams = false` to `lahore`/`karachi`/`islamabad`, `hajj-2027/page.tsx`, `umrah-visa/page.tsx`, `hotels/[city]/page.tsx` for `makkah`/`madinah`, `ziyarat/page.tsx`, `ziyarat/[city]/page.tsx` for `makkah`/`madinah`, `about/page.tsx`, `faq/page.tsx`, `contact/page.tsx`). Keep `/` focused on Entity authority, the Custom Journey Builder, and curated spoke summary gateways with crawlable `<Link href="...">` CTAs.
- Test to add: Playwright/Vitest route smoke suite asserting HTTP `200`, unique `<title>`, unique `<h1>`, self-referencing `<link rel="canonical">`, and `>=50%` non-boilerplate body text differentiation across all indexable hub and spoke routes, plus HTTP `404` for unsupported city slugs (e.g., `/umrah-packages/from-peshawar`).
- Effort: L (>1d)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### S05-002 — `PublicHeader` and `PublicFooter` emit 16 `/#hash` links, duplicate anchor destinations, and expose `/admin` in public crawl paths
- Severity: P1
- Category: seo
- Where: `web/components/layout/public-header.tsx:44–51`, `web/components/layout/public-footer.tsx:179–279`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None
- Evidence:
  1. `PublicHeader` (`web/components/layout/public-header.tsx:44–51`) defines all 6 `NAV_LINKS` as fragment URLs: `/#journey-builder`, `/#group-packages`, `/#hajj-inquiry`, `/#ziyarat`, `/#track-status`, `/#contact`.
  2. `PublicFooter` (`web/components/layout/public-footer.tsx:179–271`) defines 5 `"Journeys & Services"` links and 5 `"Sacred Destinations"` links solely as `/#...` anchors. Four distinct anchor labels — `"Custom Umrah Builder"` (`L180`), `"Instant Visa Quoting"` (`L242`), `"Makkah Clock Tower Hotels"` (`L250`), and `"Madinah Markaziah Stays"` (`L258`) — all point to the exact same `/#journey-builder` href, diluting anchor-text semantics. Two more labels (`"Fixed Group Departures"` `L187` and `"Ramadan 2026 Packages"` `L234`) both point to `/#group-packages` (and `"Ramadan 2026"` is seasonally stale in late 2026).
  3. `PublicFooter` (`L273–278`) renders `<Link href="/admin">Staff Operations Portal</Link>` on every public page without `rel="nofollow"`, directing search crawlers into an auth-gated redirect (`/admin/login`).
- Impact (business terms): Header and footer navigation carry the highest sitewide internal PageRank weight (`nextjs-business-seo-geo-auditor` Pillar 6). Pointing all 16 commercial nav/footer links back to `/#...` collapses the internal link graph into a single self-loop on `/` and wastes crawl budget on `/admin`.
- Root cause: Navigation constants were hardcoded to homepage `#hash` anchors before dedicated spoke routes existed, and staff portal convenience access was placed in the public footer.
- Recommended fix: Update `PublicHeader` and `PublicFooter` (`F-SEO-SHELL`) to link directly to clean canonical hub/spoke URLs (`/umrah-packages`, `/umrah-packages/from-lahore`, `/umrah-packages/from-karachi`, `/umrah-packages/from-islamabad`, `/hajj-2027`, `/umrah-visa`, `/hotels/makkah`, `/hotels/madinah`, `/ziyarat`, `/ziyarat/makkah`, `/ziyarat/madinah`, `/about`, `/faq`, `/contact`), replace stale `"Ramadan 2026 Packages"` copy, and either remove the public `/admin` link or add `rel="nofollow"` (coupled with `Disallow: /admin` in `app/robots.ts`).
- Test to add: Unit/DOM test on `PublicHeader` and `PublicFooter` verifying every commercial `<a href>` resolves to a clean indexable path (only `#journey-builder` / `#track-status` allowed where intentional) and no `/admin` link is followed by crawlers.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### S05-004 — `PlannerProvider` ignores URL query parameters (`?preset=`, `?departureCity=`, `?makkahHotel=`), blocking seamless Spoke-to-Builder conversion handoffs
- Severity: P2
- Category: seo
- Where: `web/components/planner/planner-context.tsx:430–597`, `web/db/seed.ts:189–205`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None
- Evidence: `PlannerProvider` (`web/components/planner/planner-context.tsx:448–462`) initializes `presetMode` unconditionally to `"complete_umrah"` and `departureCityId` to `catalog.departureCities[0]?.id ?? ""` (which resolves to Islamabad `ISB` because `ISB` has `sortOrder: 1` in `web/db/seed.ts:192`, even for visitors arriving from a Lahore or Karachi spoke!), then restores only `sessionStorage.getItem("al_ramlah_planner_draft_v1")` (`L528–567`). It never inspects URL search parameters (`?preset=visa_only`, `?departureCity=LHE`, `?makkahHotel=<id>`, `?madinahHotel=<id>`), nor does it capture inbound `utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign` / `landing_page` attribution for organic spoke conversions.
- Impact (business terms): When a pilgrim lands organically on `/umrah-visa` and clicks `"Apply for Visa Only"`, or lands on `/umrah-packages/from-lahore` and clicks `"Build Custom Package from Lahore"`, landing on `/?...#journey-builder` resets them back to `"complete_umrah"` departing from `ISB`. This friction breaks intent continuity between SEO spokes and the primary revenue conversion funnel, and leaves admins blind to which SEO spoke generated a custom quote dossier.
- Root cause: `PlannerProvider` was designed when `/` was the sole entry page and state only lived in React + `sessionStorage`.
- Recommended fix: Add safe URL search-param prefill hydration in `PlannerProvider` (`F-SEO-SHELL`, wrapped cleanly in `<Suspense>` per Next.js 16 `useSearchParams` rules or passed from `app/(public)/page.tsx` async `searchParams` Promise): support `?preset=visa_only|complete_umrah|hotels_transport`, `?departureCity=LHE|KHI|ISB` (matching `airportCode` case-insensitively), and optional `?makkahHotel=` / `?madinahHotel=` IDs when no active user-edited draft overrides them, plus persist first-touch UTM/referrer/landing-spoke metadata for lead submission.
- Test to add: Vitest/RTL or Playwright test navigating to `/?preset=visa_only&departureCity=LHE#journey-builder` and asserting `PlannerProvider` activates `visa_only` mode and selects Lahore (`LHE`).
- Effort: M (<1d)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### S05-005 — Five of eight seeded `departure_cities` (`PEW`, `MUX`, `SKT`, `LYP`, `UET`) have zero departure inventory and would trigger thin doorway penalties if programmatically generated
- Severity: P2
- Category: seo
- Where: `web/db/schema/catalog.ts:144–165`, `web/db/seed.ts:187–236`, `web/db/seed.ts:2077–2185`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None
- Evidence: `web/db/seed.ts:187–236` seeds 8 `departure_cities`: `ISB` (Islamabad), `LHE` (Lahore), `KHI` (Karachi), `PEW` (Peshawar), `MUX` (Multan), `SKT` (Sialkot), `LYP` (Faisalabad), and `UET` (Quetta). However, `departure_cities` (`web/db/schema/catalog.ts:144–165`) stores only 3 descriptive columns (`cityName`, `airportCode`, `airportName`), and `web/db/seed.ts:2077–2185` seeds `group_departures` **exclusively** for `ISB`, `LHE`, and `KHI` (0 batches for `PEW`, `MUX`, `SKT`, `LYP`, `UET`).
- Impact (business terms): Naively iterating `SELECT * FROM departure_cities WHERE is_active = true` to generate `/umrah-packages/from-[city]` for all 8 cities would publish 5 near-identical empty pages (`from-peshawar`, `from-multan`, `from-sialkot`, `from-faisalabad`, `from-quetta`) differing only by swapped city/airport strings — a textbook Google SpamBrain doorway-page violation (`entity-nap-and-programmatic-seo.md` §3–4) that demotes the entire `/umrah-packages/*` directory.
- Root cause: `departure_cities` serves the Custom Journey Builder dropdown (where custom quotes can price flights from secondary airports on request) rather than acting as a curated list of fixed-group departure hubs with substantive content.
- Recommended fix: Restrict `/umrah-packages/from-[city]` via explicit allowlist (`lahore`, `karachi`, `islamabad` with `dynamicParams = false` in `app/(public)/umrah-packages/from-[city]/page.tsx`) until a secondary gateway accumulates real `group_departures` inventory AND curated gateway logistics content (`>=50%` unique differentiation). Mention secondary gateways (`PEW`, `MUX`, `SKT`, `LYP`, `UET`) inside `/umrah-packages` FAQ / Custom Builder callout instead of spinning up thin URLs.
- Test to add: Unit test on `CITY_SPOKE_CONFIG` / `generateStaticParams` verifying only `["lahore", "karachi", "islamabad"]` are generated and each city config supplies unique airport terminal, airline routing, passport submission logistics, and FAQ data.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no
