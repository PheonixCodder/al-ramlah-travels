## 1. Executive Summary

Al Ramlah Travels sells high-trust, high-ticket religious journeys (`PKR 280,000` to `PKR 4,450,000+`), placing the domain squarely under Google's **YMYL (Your Money or Your Life)** E-E-A-T evaluation and Lahore **Local Pack** entity verification. - **HQ Address**: `Office No. 38, Street 1, Sector C, Askari 10, Airport Road, Lahore Cantt, Pakistan`

1. **No standalone `/about` or `/contact` routes exist (`404`)**, leaving Google Local Pack, Google Business Profile (GBP) website validation, and YMYL trust raters with only a homepage `#contact` scroll fragment.
2. **Phone format drift + broken international `tel:` URIs**: `db/seed.ts` and `db/schema/settings.ts` store domestic `03274029701` / `03190462638`, which `getCompanyInfo()` passes straight into Header, Footer, and Contact UI — emitting `tel:03274029701` (invalid RFC 3966 local format that fails for UK/US/CA/GCC diaspora pilgrims) while `DEFAULT_COMPANY_INFO` uses `+92 327 4029701`.
3. **Office address suffix mismatch**: `lib/company-info.ts` omits `, Pakistan` while `lib/constants/brand.ts`, `db/schema/settings.ts`, and `db/seed.ts` include `, Pakistan`.
4. **Fragmented `agencySettings` loading & social URL drift**: `loadVerifiedCompanyInfo()` is locked inside `lib/whatsapp/outbox.ts`, legal pages query `agencySettings` without `try/catch`, `DEFAULT_COMPANY_INFO` nulls out all 4 social URLs despite `BRAND_SOCIAL_URLS`, and `app/(public)/layout.tsx` bypasses `getCompanyInfo()` for footer socials.

## 3. Detailed Findings

### S10-001 — Missing standalone `/about` and `/contact` pages blocks Lahore Local Pack ranking and YMYL E-E-A-T verification
- Severity: P1
- Category: seo
- Where: `app/(public)/` directory (only `page.tsx`, `privacy/`, `terms/`, `refund-policy/`, `voucher/[token]/` exist), `components/layout/public-header.tsx:46-51`, `components/layout/public-footer.tsx:58-64`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None
- Evidence: Listing `app/(public)` confirms neither `app/(public)/about/page.tsx` nor `app/(public)/contact/page.tsx` exists — requesting `/about` or `/contact` returns HTTP `404` (`app/not-found.tsx`). Both `PublicHeader` (`public-header.tsx:50`: `{ label: "Concierge", href: "/#contact" }`) and `PublicFooter` (`public-footer.tsx:63`: `{ label: "Contact Office", href: "/#contact" }`) point to a URL hash fragment `/#contact`, which search engines canonicalize to `/` and never index as a dedicated URL.
- Impact (business terms): High-intent brand, trust, and local-pack searches (`"Al Ramlah Travels Lahore"`, `"Al Ramlah Travels Askari 10 contact number"`, `"Umrah travel agency near Lahore Airport Road"`, `"is Al Ramlah Travels registered"`) lack dedicated landing pages. For YMYL transactions where Pakistani families wire `PKR 500,000–4,000,000+` for visas and Haramain hotels, missing `/about` and `/contact` pages severely depresses both human conversion confidence and Google E-E-A-T / Google Business Profile authority.
- Root cause: Initial storefront MVP consolidated marketing and lead capture onto a single scrolling page (`app/(public)/page.tsx`).
- Recommended fix: Create server-rendered `app/(public)/about/page.tsx` and `app/(public)/contact/page.tsx` (owned by `F-SEO-TRUST`) and update `PublicHeader` + `PublicFooter` links (`F-SEO-SHELL`):
  1. **`/about`**: Heritage & Haramain concierge philosophy (truthful copy per `OD-10` — zero fabricated headcounts/years), Askari 10 Lahore Cantt physical headquarters details, KSA Masar/Nusuk operational workflow, dynamic `DTS / MoRA / IATA / NTN` & `legalEntityName` verification block (rendered strictly when populated in `agencySettings`), pilgrimage service coverage (Lahore `LHE`, Karachi `KHI`, Islamabad `ISB`, Multan, Faisalabad, Sialkot + UK/US/CA/GCC diaspora), and `TravelAgency` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD.
  2. **`/contact`**: Full canonical NAP card, 1-click E.164 `tel:+923274029701` / `tel:+923190462638` and WhatsApp `wa.me/923274029701` CTAs, Mon–Sat `09:00 AM – 08:00 PM PKT` desk hours, physical visiting guide from Allama Iqbal International Airport (`LHE`, ~5 mins via Airport Road to Sector C, Askari 10), verified Google Maps embed/link, interactive inquiry form (`ContactSection`), and `TravelAgency` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD.
- Test to add: Integration / route test `tests/seo/trust-pages.test.ts` verifying `/about` and `/contact` export unique `50–60` char titles, `150–160` char descriptions, canonical URLs, identical canonical NAP strings, and valid `TravelAgency` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD.

- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### S10-002 — Phone format split (`03274029701` vs `+92 327 4029701`) and invalid non-E.164 `tel:` links break NAP consistency and diaspora calling
- Severity: P1
- Category: seo
- Where: `lib/company-info.ts:127-128, 175-178`, `db/schema/settings.ts:27-30`, `db/seed.ts:89-90`, `components/layout/public-header.tsx:79-80`, `components/layout/public-footer.tsx:82-83`, `components/public/contact-section.tsx:217, 232`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None (`OD-10` specifies `+92 327 4029701` and `+92 319 0462638`)
- Evidence:
  1. `db/schema/settings.ts:27-30` defaults `primaryWhatsapp` to `"03274029701"` and `secondaryPhone` to `"03190462638"`, and `db/seed.ts:89-90` seeds those exact local strings into PostgreSQL.
  2. `getCompanyInfo()` (`lib/company-info.ts:175-178`) converts `whatsappE164` via `normalizeWhatsAppDigits()` (`"923274029701"`), **but** assigns `primaryWhatsapp = normalizeNonEmpty(dbSettings?.primaryWhatsapp) ?? DEFAULT_COMPANY_INFO.primaryWhatsapp` without international display formatting. Thus, whenever DB settings exist, the live site displays `03274029701` and `03190462638`, whereas DB-fallback renders `+92 327 4029701` and `+92 319 0462638` (`docs/audit/seo/baseline/nap_audit.json` already flagged `4` phone variants across 10 files).
  3. `PublicHeader` (`public-header.tsx:80`), `PublicFooter` (`public-footer.tsx:82-83`), and `ContactSection` (`contact-section.tsx:217, 232`) construct `tel:` URIs with `tel:${phone.replace(/\s+/g, "")}`, producing `href="tel:03274029701"` and `href="tel:03190462638"`. Per RFC 3966 §5.1.5, global `tel:` URIs must use `+` E.164 (`tel:+923274029701`).
- Impact (business terms):
  1. **Lost high-value diaspora leads**: Overseas Pakistani pilgrims in the UK, USA, Canada, UAE, and KSA tapping `"Call"` in Header/Footer/Contact dial a domestic `0327...` trunk code that fails immediately on foreign carriers.
  2. **Local SEO NAP dilution**: Emitting `03274029701` in HTML text, `+92 327 4029701` in metadata/fallbacks, and `923274029701` in WhatsApp links splits citation signals across Google Business Profile and directory crawlers.
- Root cause: `lib/company-info.ts` normalizes digits for `wa.me/` (`normalizeWhatsAppDigits`) but lacks `formatPkDisplayPhone()` (`+92 3XX XXXXXXX`) and `formatTelHref()` (`tel:+923XXXXXXXXX`) helpers.
- Recommended fix: Add `formatPkPhoneDisplay(raw)` (`"03274029701"` -> `"+92 327 4029701"`) and `formatPhoneTelHref(raw)` (`"03274029701"` -> `"tel:+923274029701"`) in `lib/company-info.ts`. Normalize `primaryWhatsapp` and `secondaryPhone` inside `getCompanyInfo()` so every consumer (Header, Footer, Contact, Legal pages, Vouchers, and `TravelAgency` JSON-LD) renders identical `+92 327 4029701` / `+92 319 0462638` display text and `tel:+923274029701` / `tel:+923190462638` links regardless of how staff typed the number in `/admin/settings`.
- Test to add: Unit test `tests/seo/company-info-nap.test.ts` proving `getCompanyInfo({ primaryWhatsapp: "03274029701", secondaryPhone: "0319-0462638" })` outputs `primaryWhatsapp === "+92 327 4029701"`, `secondaryPhone === "+92 319 0462638"`, and `tel:` hrefs `"tel:+923274029701"` and `"tel:+923190462638"`.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no (resolved by `OD-10`)

### S10-003 — HQ street address string drifts between `lib/company-info.ts` (no `, Pakistan`) and `lib/constants/brand.ts` / `db/seed.ts` (with `, Pakistan`)
- Severity: P2
- Category: seo
- Where: `lib/company-info.ts:76-77`, `lib/constants/brand.ts:16-17`, `db/schema/settings.ts:36-37`, `db/seed.ts:80`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None (`OD-10` canonicalizes HQ address)
- Evidence: `lib/company-info.ts:76-77` defines `DEFAULT_OFFICE_ADDRESS = "Office No. 38, Street 1, Sector C, Askari 10, Airport Road, Lahore Cantt"`, whereas `lib/constants/brand.ts:16-17` (`BRAND_OFFICE_ADDRESS`), `db/schema/settings.ts:37`, and `db/seed.ts:80` define `"Office No. 38, Street 1, Sector C, Askari 10, Airport Road, Lahore Cantt, Pakistan"`. If `agencySettings` is empty or unreachable during static generation/fallback, the rendered address drops `, Pakistan`.
- Impact (business terms): Datawise Local SEO Lesson 2 requires 100% character-for-character NAP consistency across config constants, Header/Footer, `/contact`, legal pages, and Google Business Profile. Even minor suffix variations between cached static builds and live DB renders weaken entity matching.

- Effort: S (<2h)

### S10-004 — Fragmented `agencySettings` queries, unguarded legal-page DB calls, and `null` social URL fallbacks break `sameAs` entity linking
- Severity: P2
- Category: reliability
- Where: `lib/whatsapp/outbox.ts:660-667`, `lib/company-info.ts:151-154`, `lib/constants/brand.ts:40-45`, `app/(public)/layout.tsx:16-24, 48-51`, `app/(public)/page.tsx:139`, `app/(public)/privacy/page.tsx:18-19`, `app/(public)/terms/page.tsx:17-18`, `app/(public)/refund-policy/page.tsx:18-19`, `db/seed.ts:106-120`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None
- Evidence:
  1. `loadVerifiedCompanyInfo()` is defined inside `lib/whatsapp/outbox.ts:660-667` rather than `lib/company-info.ts`.
  2. Public pages execute three divergent DB patterns: `app/(public)/layout.tsx:18-20` queries `where(eq(id, "default"))` inside `try/catch`; `app/(public)/page.tsx:139` calls `db.query.agencySettings.findFirst().catch(() => null)`; and `privacy/page.tsx:18`, `terms/page.tsx:17`, and `refund-policy/page.tsx:18` run raw `await db.select().from(schema.agencySettings).limit(1)` **with zero `try/catch`** (crashing with HTTP `500` during transient Neon cold-start errors instead of falling back to `DEFAULT_COMPANY_INFO`).
  3. `DEFAULT_COMPANY_INFO` (`lib/company-info.ts:151-154`) sets `facebookUrl`, `instagramUrl`, `youtubeUrl`, and `tiktokUrl` to `null` instead of `BRAND_SOCIAL_URLS` (`lib/constants/brand.ts:40-45`). Meanwhile, `app/(public)/layout.tsx:48-51` passes `settings?.facebookUrl ?? null` to `<PublicFooter>` instead of `info.facebookUrl`, and `db/seed.ts:106-120` omits the 4 social URL columns in `.onConflictDoUpdate` (so re-seeding an older DB row leaves social URLs `null`).
- Impact (business terms): Missing or `null` social URLs cause `<PublicFooter>` social icons and `TravelAgency.sameAs` JSON-LD links to vanish silently in fallback or re-seeded environments, severing cross-platform entity verification across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok. Unguarded legal-page queries also risk `500` crawl errors when Googlebot crawls `/privacy`, `/terms`, or `/refund-policy`.
- Root cause: `getCompanyInfo()` was never paired with a single shared server loader (`loadPublicCompanyInfo()`) or wired to `BRAND_SOCIAL_URLS` defaults.
- Recommended fix:
  1. Add `loadVerifiedCompanyInfo()` (cached per request via `React.cache()`) in `lib/company-info.ts` (or `lib/seo/public-queries.ts`) with resilient `try/catch` fallback to `DEFAULT_COMPANY_INFO`, and replace all ad-hoc `agencySettings` queries in `app/(public)/**`.
  2. Populate `DEFAULT_COMPANY_INFO` social URLs from canonicalized `https://www.` `BRAND_SOCIAL_URLS`, pass `info.facebookUrl` (etc.) in `app/(public)/layout.tsx`, and include social URL fields in `db/seed.ts` `.onConflictDoUpdate` only when unset or explicitly seeded.
- Test to add: Unit test verifying `getCompanyInfo(null)` includes valid `https://` `BRAND_SOCIAL_URLS` and `loadVerifiedCompanyInfo()` gracefully returns `DEFAULT_COMPANY_INFO` when the DB query rejects.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no
