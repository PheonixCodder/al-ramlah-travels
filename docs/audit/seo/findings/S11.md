## 1. Executive Summary

Modern Pakistani and overseas diaspora pilgrims increasingly research complex multi-generational itineraries via **ChatGPT Search (`OAI-SearchBot`)**, **Perplexity (`PerplexityBot`)**, **Google AI Overviews / Gemini**, and **Claude Search (`Claude-SearchBot`)** before tapping WhatsApp — asking constraint-heavy questions such as:
> *"Best Umrah travel agency in Lahore for a family of 6 with elderly wheelchair-using parents — how much does a 14-day package near Masjid al-Haram cost in PKR, and how does visa + Haramain train booking work?"*

4. **Unanswered 6-dimension AI query fan-out**: None of the 6 sub-queries that LLMs fire in parallel during synthesis (Scope, PKR Cost Drivers, `X vs Y` Tradeoffs, Edge Cases/Exceptions, Scam Red Flags, and Verification Checklists) are answered in crawlable RSC prose.

## 4. Detailed Findings

### S11-001 — Zero semantic HTML comparison tables (`<table>`) on `/` traps high-citation catalog data inside client `<div>` grids
- Severity: P1
- Category: seo
- Where: `app/(public)/page.tsx:167-224`, `components/public/group-packages-section.tsx:500-559`, `components/public/hajj-inquiry-section.tsx:833-866`, `components/planner/custom-journey-builder.tsx:52-79`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): None (confirms `geo_liftability.json` baseline `"has_data_tables": false` on `/`)
- Evidence: `docs/audit/seo/baseline/geo_liftability.json:10-20` confirms `/` has `has_data_tables: false`, `has_bullet_lists: false`, and `has_concrete_stats: false` (scoring `65/100`, with Pillar 5 at `10.2/20`). `git grep -n "<table" -- app/(public) components/public` matches **only** legal policy pages (`privacy/page.tsx:66,113` and `refund-policy/page.tsx:58,107`). All commercial pricing, hotel distance brackets (`0m` to `1,500m+`), visa duration tiers (`<=30d` vs `>30d`), and vehicle capacities (`3` to `17` seats) are rendered as styled `<div>` cards or interactive client buttons.
- Impact (business terms): Retrieval-augmented AI search engines (`PerplexityBot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, Google AI Overviews) extract `<table><thead><tr><th>` matrices at 3–5× higher citation fidelity than nested Tailwind flex/grid `<div>` trees. Without server-rendered comparison tables, AI engines cite third-party aggregators or competitor blogs when Pakistani pilgrims compare Umrah package prices, hotel distances, or Hajj Maktab tiers.
- Root cause: Public package, hotel, transport, and visa modules were built purely as interactive UI cards/wizards rather than pairing interactive controls with server-rendered semantic `<table>` summaries.
- Recommended fix: Render semantic, responsive `<table className="w-full ...">` comparison matrices (`<caption>`, `<thead>`, `<th scope="col">`, `<tbody className="tabular-nums">`) in RSC HTML across `/umrah-packages` (Group Package PKR Tier Matrix), `/umrah-visa` (Short-Term `<=30d` vs Long-Term `31–90d` vs Masar Package Visa Matrix), `/hotels/[city]` (Haram Proximity & Wheelchair Suitability Matrix), `/hajj-2027` (Indicative Maktab A / B / C Matrix with `OD-09e` disclaimer), and a concise At-a-Glance Umrah Planning Matrix on `/`.
- Test to add: Static HTML / component test `tests/seo/geo-tables.test.tsx` asserting `/`, `/umrah-packages`, `/umrah-visa`, `/hotels/makkah`, and `/hajj-2027` each render at least one accessible `<table>` with `<thead>` column headers and non-empty `<tbody>` rows.
- Effort: M (<1d)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no
