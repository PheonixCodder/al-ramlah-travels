## 2. Why `/#fragment` Links Fail Crawl Discovery & Topical Authority

1. **HTTP Request Stripping (RFC 3986 §3.5):** Browsers and search crawlers strip everything from `#` onward before issuing an HTTP `GET`. Crawling `/#group-packages`, `/#hajj-inquiry`, `/#ziyarat`, `/#faq`, and `/#contact` produces five identical `GET / HTTP/1.1` requests returning the exact same `<title>`, `<h1>`, and canonical URL (`https://www.alramlahtravels.com/`).
2. **Anchor-Text Cannibalization on `/`:** In [public-footer.tsx](file:///c:/dev/al-ramlah-travels/web/components/layout/public-footer.tsx#L179-L230), four distinct commercial intents — `"Custom Umrah Builder"`, `"Instant Visa Quoting"`, `"Makkah Clock Tower Hotels"`, and `"Madinah Markaziah Stays"` — all point to `/#journey-builder`, while `"VIP Executive Packages"` and `"Family Comfort Umrah"` both point to `/#group-packages`. Google treats multiple links to the same canonical target on one page under first-anchor/diluted-anchor rules, preventing any page from ranking specifically for *"Umrah visa price Pakistan"*, *"Makkah Clock Tower hotels from Pakistan"*, or *"Umrah packages from Lahore"*.
3. **Zero Sitelinks & AI Search Citation Loss:** Google Sitelinks and AI answer engines (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) prefer citing dedicated, topically scoped URLs (`/umrah-visa`, `/hajj-2027`, `/hotels/makkah`) rather than an overloaded 900-line homepage DOM containing 6 unrelated services.
