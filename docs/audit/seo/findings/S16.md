### S16-001 — Zero JSON-LD structured data across 100% of public routes (`0/29` routes) and no XSS-safe Rich-Results builder library
- Severity: P0
- Category: seo
- Where: `app/layout.tsx:40-70`, `app/(public)/page.tsx:53-377`, `app/(public)/privacy/page.tsx`, `app/(public)/terms/page.tsx`, `app/(public)/refund-policy/page.tsx` (`docs/audit/seo/baseline/codebase_audit.json:10` -> `"routes_with_jsonld": 0`)
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): `Spec 0025` readiness scope requiring truthful entity-consistent JSON-LD (`TravelAgency`, `Offer`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`).
- Evidence: Running `uv run nextjs_seo_geo_cli.py audit-codebase` and `check-geo-liftability` confirms `"routes_with_jsonld": 0` and `"has_json_ld_schema": false` on every route in `web/app/`. Neither `app/layout.tsx` nor `app/(public)/page.tsx` renders `<script type="application/ld+json">`. Google Search, Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, and Perplexity receive zero machine-readable entity identity, zero `PostalAddress` (`Office No. 38, Street 1, Sector C, Askari 10, Airport Road, Lahore Cantt`), zero `FAQPage` entities, zero `BreadcrumbList` hierarchy, and zero `TouristTrip` / `Offer` pricing for fixed Umrah group packages.
- Impact (business terms): Complete loss of Google Rich Results eligibility (FAQ rich snippets, breadcrumb trails, local travel agency knowledge panel, and package price snippets) and severe entity disambiguation weakness in AI answer engines for Lahore/Pakistan Umrah & Hajj queries.
- Root cause: Slices `1`–`43` focused on functional vertical slices and Phase A security/reliability hardening without a `lib/seo/json-ld.ts` structured data layer.
- Recommended fix: Create `lib/seo/json-ld.ts` exporting deterministic, strictly typed builders (`buildTravelAgencyJsonLd`, `buildWebSiteJsonLd`, `buildBreadcrumbJsonLd`, `buildFaqPageJsonLd`, `buildUmrahPackageJsonLd`, `buildVisaServiceJsonLd`, `buildHajjServiceJsonLd`, `buildHotelItemListJsonLd`, `buildZiyaratItemListJsonLd`) plus a server `<JsonLd />` component that serializes `JSON.stringify(payload).replace(/</g, "\\u003c")` to prevent `</script>` XSS breakout.
- Test to add: `tests/seo/json-ld-and-metadata.test.ts` — validates every builder output against strict Schema.org Zod contracts, verifies `<` -> `\u003c` XSS escaping on malicious CMS strings, and asserts `100%` NAP identity match against `getCompanyInfo()`.
- Effort: M (<1d)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### S16-002 — High risk of Google Rich-Results manual penalty if structured data emits `AggregateRating` with `0` verified reviews or fixed `Offer` prices on volatile/indicative services
- Severity: P0
- Category: seo
- Where: `db/seed.ts:2325` (`0` seeded testimonials per `OD-10`), `db/schema/packages.ts:169-214` (`hajjPackages.isIndicative = true` per `OD-09e`), `lib/domain/visa-calc.ts` (Invariant #2 volatile vs fixed PKR)
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): N/A — preventative Rich-Results compliance & Haramain Business Invariant #2 / `OD-09e` / `OD-10` enforcement.
- Evidence: In `db/seed.ts:2325`, fake testimonials were rightly purged in Wave 3 (`0` rows in `testimonials` by default per `OD-10`), and `hajjPackages` (`db/schema/packages.ts:189-194`) carries `isIndicative: true` with `regulatoryDisclaimer` per `OD-09e`. Furthermore, Business Invariant #2 mandates `"Live Rate — Confirmed upon submission"` whenever volatile services (`Flights`, `Hotels`, `Transport`, `Ziyarat`) are custom-selected. Naive SEO templates frequently hardcode `AggregateRating: { ratingValue: "4.9", reviewCount: "150" }` or attach fixed `Offer.price` to indicative/volatile services — both trigger Google Search Console "Review snippet / Merchant listing structured data" manual actions and violate `OD-09e` / `OD-10`.
- Impact (business terms): Sitewide Google Rich Results suppression (manual spam action) and legal/commercial misrepresentation of volatile Umrah or indicative Hajj 2027 tariffs.
- Root cause: Tension between generic e-commerce Schema.org templates and regulated Pakistani pilgrimage pricing invariants unless guarded in code.
- Recommended fix: Enforce three hard guards inside `lib/seo/json-ld.ts`:
  1. Never emit `aggregateRating` or `review` unless verified active DB `testimonials` rows are visibly rendered on that exact page (`testimonials.length > 0`).
  2. Emit fixed `Offer` / `AggregateOffer` (`priceCurrency: "PKR"`) **strictly** on (a) fixed `groupPackages` (`sharingPricePkr`, `quadPricePkr`, `triplePricePkr`, `doublePricePkr`) with truthful `availability` (`https://schema.org/InStock` when open upcoming seats `> 0`, `https://schema.org/LimitedAvailability` on `filling_fast`, `https://schema.org/SoldOut` when `0` open seats) and (b) `Visa Only` regulatory tiers on `/umrah-visa` (`visaRates.pricePkr`).
  3. Represent `/hajj-2027` using `Service` / `TouristTrip` without `InStock` checkout `Offer` claims whenever `isIndicative === true`, embedding the `OD-09e` MORA/Nusuk disclaimer in `description` and visible UI.
- Test to add: `tests/seo/json-ld-and-metadata.test.ts` — asserts `aggregateRating` is `undefined` when `testimonials = []`, asserts `hajjPackages` with `isIndicative: true` never emit `InStock` fixed-price `Offer` nodes, and asserts sold-out group packages emit `https://schema.org/SoldOut`.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no

### S16-004 — `proxy.ts` omits `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` HTTP header on `/voucher/[token]` (and `/quote/*`) capability routes
- Severity: P1
- Category: seo
- Where: `proxy.ts:83-85`, `app/(public)/voucher/[token]/page.tsx:27-30`
- Brief claim contradicted (if any): `OD-04` / `OD-06` capability-token privacy protection against accidental search indexing.
- Evidence: `proxy.ts:83-85` sets `response.headers.set("X-Robots-Tag", "noindex, nofollow")` only when `pathname.startsWith("/admin") || pathname.startsWith("/api")`. While `app/(public)/voucher/[token]/page.tsx:27-30` sets HTML `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`, capability URLs shared via email/WhatsApp or link unfurlers benefit from defense-in-depth HTTP header `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow, noarchive` at the edge proxy layer (especially on `403`/`404` or non-HTML responses).
- Impact (business terms): Capability-signed `/voucher/[token]` URLs lack HTTP-level `X-Robots-Tag` protection against non-HTML crawlers and snippet caching (`noarchive`).
- Root cause: `proxy.ts` predated `/voucher/[token]` (`T-017`/`T-023`) and only matched `/admin` and `/api`.
- Recommended fix: Update `proxy.ts:83-85` (`F-SEO-PLAT`) so `/admin`, `/api`, `/voucher`, and `/quote` responses all emit `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` (and `noarchive, nosnippet` on `/voucher` & `/quote`), while keeping public indexable routes free of `X-Robots-Tag` restrictions.
- Test to add: `tests/e2e/j23-seo-crawl-and-attribution.spec.ts` — asserts `GET /admin/login`, `GET /api/health`, and `GET /voucher/invalid-or-valid` return HTTP header `x-robots-tag` containing `noindex, nofollow`, whereas `GET /` and `GET /umrah-packages` do NOT have `noindex`.
- Effort: S (<2h)
- Confidence: High
- Needs owner decision: no
