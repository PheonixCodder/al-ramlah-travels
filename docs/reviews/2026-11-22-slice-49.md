## Minor

### 🟡 Admin reupload requests are not blocked on purged document slots, `app/actions/pilgrim-documents.ts:2104`
**Problem**: In `reviewPilgrimDocumentAction`, lines 2104 to 2120 reject `"approved"` decisions when `docRow.status === "purged"`, but allow `decision === "reupload_requested"` to proceed against a `"purged"` document row. Because `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` at line 2076 also omits `pii_purged_at`, calling `reviewPilgrimDocumentAction` with `reupload_requested` on a purged slot can overwrite `status` from `"purged"` back to `"reupload_requested"`.
**Why it matters**: While `pii_purged_at` remains non null so pilgrim uploads are still blocked by `409 SLOT_PURGED_LOCKED`, flipping `status` out of `"purged"` breaks the terminal retention state and skews progress counts in `loadHydratedTravellersAndProgress` (`purgedCount` vs `reuploadRequestedCount`).
**Suggested fix**: Include `pii_purged_at` in the `FOR UPDATE` query at `app/actions/pilgrim-documents.ts:2076` and return `409 SLOT_PURGED_LOCKED` at the start of `reviewPilgrimDocumentAction` for both `"approved"` and `"reupload_requested"` whenever `docRow.status === "purged" || docRow.pii_purged_at !== null`.
