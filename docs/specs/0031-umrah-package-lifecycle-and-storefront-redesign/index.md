## Summary

Today, creating a Group Umrah package in `/admin/studio` forces operations staff past stacked marketing banners into separate modals (`Create Package` then 3× `Add Batch`), silently hiding newly created packages that have zero batches, while publishing a card from the AI Poster Studio copies vertical text-heavy 4K social posters directly into `group_packages.bannerUrl`. On the public storefront, `/umrah-packages` and `/umrah-packages/from-[city]` lead with a 920px spreadsheet table (with zero visual package cards on city spokes), and `/umrah-packages/[slug]` forces pilgrims through double-modal friction without showing Makkah/Madinah hotel photos or live family pricing.

Slice 51 cleanly separates **public website cover photography (`cover_image_url` + automatic linked hotel photo fallback)** from **internal campaign posters (`internal_poster_url`)**, transforms `/admin/studio` into a **Package-First `Operate` Workbench** with a **3-Step Slide-Over Builder** (creating a package + `LHE`/`KHI`/`ISB` batches atomically in one pass + 1-click batch cloning), introduces a unified **Visual Package Explorer** across `/umrah-packages` and `/umrah-packages/from-[city]`, and upgrades `/umrah-packages/[slug]` with a **Dual-Sanctuary Hotel Showcase, Step-by-Step Pilgrimage Timeline, and Live Sticky Family Price Calculator** with inline `ART-...` seat reservation.

## Requirements

**User stories**:
- As **Lahore Operations Staff (`staff` or `super_admin`)**, I want `/admin/studio` to open directly onto a scannable Umrah Packages & Batches inventory with live storefront visibility badges, 1-click `Clone Batch to KHI/ISB`, and a 3-step Slide-Over Builder that creates a package and its regional batches together while keeping internal 4K WhatsApp/social posters completely separate from website card photography.
- As a **Pakistani or Diaspora Family Pilgrim**, I want to filter Umrah packages instantly by Departure City (`LHE`, `KHI`, `ISB`), Room Occupancy (`Sharing`, `Quad`, `Triple`, `Double`), Tier, and Haram Walking Access on `/umrah-packages` and `/umrah-packages/from-[city]`, and calculate my exact `Adults + Children + Infants` PKR total inline on `/umrah-packages/[slug]` without reopening modals.
